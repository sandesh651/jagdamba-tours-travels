GOOD TO KNOW

A few things before you go.

What does the ₹5,999 package include?

It is an indicative starting price for this concept. No accommodation, transport, meals, activity fees or taxes are promised yet. Jagdamba will confirm the complete inclusions, exclusions and total before a booking.

Are all five places covered in one trip?

Each circuit suggests five stops within a regional cluster. The final duration, order and feasibility depend on your departure point, road conditions, opening hours and available time.

Can we visit Mhatari chi Chul?

We can include it as a requested addition to the South Konkan circuit once the local operator confirms the exact location, access, sea conditions and any extra boat charges. It is not a guaranteed stop in this preview.

Can we customise the trip or request only a vehicle?

Yes, use the custom trip or vehicle enquiry option to describe what you need. Availability, vehicle type and charges must be confirmed by Jagdamba.

Are boat rides and water sports guaranteed?

No. Sea access, boat rides, fort ferries and water sports depend on weather, tide, local permissions and operator availability. Activities and charges require separate confirmation.
